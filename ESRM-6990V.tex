\section{TIMSS DATA}\label{timss-data}

\subsection{Introduction: Research Scenario and Research
Questions}\label{introduction-research-scenario-and-research-questions}

The researcher would want to investigate the performance of students in
the United States of America (USA) and Canada based on the 2023
\href{https://timss2023.org/data}{TIMSS} (Trends in International
Mathematics and Science Study) dataset. This analysis focuses on
\textbf{math achievements} of 4th grade students using multiple
\textbf{plausible values} and considering relevant predictors including
\textbf{socioeconomic status (SES)}, \textbf{parental education}, and
\textbf{gender}. Specifically, this research;

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Explores how factors such as SES, gender, country, student motivation
  among others affect the performance of a student. (Can do sensitivity
  analysis with SES and etc) Limitation: Missing values - future work
  focuses on multiple imputation.
\item
  Examines how various teacher characteristics influence student's math
  achievements.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Data}\label{data}

\subsubsection{Data Preparation}\label{data-preparation}

The data preparation stage begins with renaming the columns in the
\texttt{Home\_Student} dataset with more descriptive names. After this,
the dataset is cleaned by removing any rows containing missing values
resulting in a final dataset with only complete cases. Following this,
the scales of several variables were reversed to ensure consistency in
their interpretation. For \textbf{Disorderly Behavior}, the scale is
reversed so that 3 now represents the least disorderly behavior, and 1
indicates the most disorderly behavior. Similarly, for \textbf{Student
Bullying}, the scale is reversed so that 3 corresponds to the least
bullying, and 1 represents the highest level of bullying. The scale for
\textbf{Instructional Clarity} is also reversed, with 3 signifying high
clarity of instruction and 1 indicating low clarity. In the case of
\textbf{Digital Self-Efficacy}, 3 now represents high self-efficacy in
digital skills, while 1 reflects low self-efficacy. The \textbf{School
Belonging} scale is reversed so that 3 indicates a strong sense of
belonging, and 1 represents a low sense of belonging. For \textbf{Like
Learning Math}, 3 now reflects a high enjoyment of learning math,
whereas 1 indicates a low enjoyment. Finally, the \textbf{Confidence in
Math} scale is reversed, with 3 representing high confidence in
mathematics and 1 signifying low confidence. These transformations make
the variables more intuitive for analysis, where higher values
consistently represent more favorable outcomes.
